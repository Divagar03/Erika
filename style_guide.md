# Zenith Motors — Automotive Edit Style Guide

## 1. Edit Philosophy
The channel must look and feel like it is run by a genuine high-end car-edit creator, not an automated bot.

## 2. Hook Rules (First 1–2 Seconds)
- **Always open with the most dramatic frame:** launch acceleration, exhaust backfire flame, high-rpm revs, high-speed flyby, or apex clip.
- **Never start with a logo, slow title screen, or static fade-in.** The opening frame must earn the stop-scroll in the first 0.5–1.5 seconds.

## 3. Cut Rhythm & Audio
- **Beat-synced cuts:** Cuts must land on the musical beat or snare hit.
- **Speed ramps:** Normal rolling speed → sudden acceleration fast-forward → slow-mo catch on the peak moment.
- **Authentic sound:** Engine symphony (V10 screaming, twin-turbo spool, V12 exhaust roar) should remain clearly audible over background beats.

## 4. 6 Edit Templates (Rotated Continuously)

### Template 1: Cinematic Slow-Mo (`cinematic_slow_mo`)
- 60fps slowed to smooth half-speed motion.
- High-contrast, warm/teal grade, focused on aero lines, wheel details, and track presence.
- Best for: Porsche 911 GT3 RS, Ferrari SF90, BMW S1000RR.

### Template 2: Speed Ramp (`speed_ramp`)
- Acceleration cuts: slow turn-in → snap acceleration → cinematic drift cut.
- High energy, bass-driven beats.
- Best for: Nissan GT-R Nismo, Lamborghini Revuelto, Audi R8 V10.

### Template 3: Phonk / Bass-Drop Edit (`phonk_bass_drop`)
- Hard cuts synced to deep 808 bass drops and distorted phonk beats.
- Gritty, nocturnal, aggressive atmosphere.
- Best for: Godzilla GT-R, Night roadsters, aggressive superbikes.

### Template 4: Night City (`night_city`)
- Neon reflections, rainy asphalt bokeh, twilight tunnels.
- Cooler cyan/purple grade.
- Best for: Tokyo/Dubai expressway aesthetic, Diablo V12, Ferrari F80.

### Template 5: Rolling Shots (`rolling_shots`)
- Smooth tracking follow shots from a chase car perspective.
- Flowing take with uninterrupted high-speed motion.
- Best for: Aston Martin Valour, Mustang GT, Ferrari SF90.

### Template 6: Engine Sound Showcase (`engine_sound_showcase`)
- Engine audio is the absolute star of the mix.
- High-revving naturally aspirated V10/V12 symphonies with minimal dialogue or text distraction.
- Best for: Audi R8 V10, Lamborghini Diablo, Ducati Panigale V4 S.

## 5. Captions & Hashtags
- **Format:** Punchy one-liners, enthusiast questions, or verified engineering facts.
- **Length:** 2–3 short sentences + engaging call-to-action.
- **Hashtags:** 6–10 relevant tags mixing broad (`#supercars`, `#superbike`), model-specific (`#gt3rs`, `#revuelto`), and community tags (`#carreels`, `#speedhunters`).
- **Fact verification:** Horsepower, 0–100 km/h, engine displacement, and prices must strictly match the verified specification database.
